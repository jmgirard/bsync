# M015: Segment-shuffling surrogate generator

**Status:** done (2026-10-07, PR #10 https://github.com/jmgirard/bsync/pull/10)

**Goal:** Add an exported segment-shuffling surrogate generator that reorders whole segments of a series in orders drawn uniformly from all orders except the original. Offer it as a `surrogate_method` in `synchrony_multiverse()` and `autotune_wcc()`, with windows that lie inside one segment.

**Outcome:** `generate_surrogate_segment(y, segment_size, n_surrogates = 100)` cuts k = floor(n / segment_size) segments from sample 1 (the SUSY 0.1.0 cut, source note `references/tschacher2020.md`) and draws distinct orders uniformly from the k! - 1 non-identity orders: listed and sampled up to 8 segments, drawn and rejected above. The tail stays in place, `NA` moves with its segment, all orders are returned when `n_surrogates >= k! - 1` (k <= 8), and k <= 3 warns that p <= .05 is unreachable. `synchrony_multiverse()` and `autotune_wcc()` take `"segment"`: segment s = window + 2 * lag (window for Granger), increment s, rows not crossed with `increment_pct` (NA), one matrix per distinct s, cells with fewer than 4 segments or s < 2 skipped with class `bsync_segment_skipped`, one `bsync_segment_aligned` message per call (autotune also aggregates skips and names dyads). The multiverse seconds-to-samples conversion is vectorized before generation, and other methods' results are `identical()` to main. Tests: `test-surrogate-segment.R` (plain reference, invariants, chi-square uniformity), `test-surrogate-calibration.R` (size 0.056 and 0.045, control 0.100), `test-multiverse-segment.R` (wrapper equalities, skips, dashboard snapshot). Docs: help page, vignette section 1.4, NEWS, README, DESIGN §2, §6, §13, §14 #8. The segment half of the "Expanded surrogate generators" row was absorbed.

**Decisions:** D-004 (uniform non-identity orders and aligned windows), from RB01/RR01 (archived in `reviews/archive/`).
