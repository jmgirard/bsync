# References index

<!-- One line per committed page under cairn/references/. -->

- kleinbub2020.md — Kleinbub & Ramseyer 2020 (rMEA, Psychotherapy Research): the `shuffle()` pseudo-dyad convention (pairing, roles, sampling, start-aligned crop) read from the rMEA 1.2.2 source, and where bsync's pseudo-dyad generators depart from it.
- phipson2010.md — Phipson & Smyth 2010 (Stat. Appl. Genet. Mol. Biol.): the add-one Monte Carlo p-value (b + 1) / (m + 1) (p. 6) that every surrogate wrapper returns, and the size of the b / m estimator (p. 4).
- lachaux1999.md — Lachaux et al. 1999 (Human Brain Mapping): the PLV definition wphase implements (p. 195 formula), phase-extraction and surrogate-test context, and bsync's stated single-trial windowed adaptation.
- schreiber1996.md — Schreiber & Schmitz 1996 (Phys. Rev. Lett.): the IAAFT iteration, its fixed-point end and accuracy stop (p. 2), the discrepancy measure (pp. 2-3), and the AR(1) cube test process that `generate_surrogate_iaaft()` and its tests use.
- tschacher2020.md — Tschacher & Meier 2020 (Psychotherapy Research): the SUSY 0.1.0 segment convention (segment count, cut from row 1, dropped tail, size rules, all-pairs surrogate set) read from the `susy()` source, and where `generate_surrogate_segment()` departs from it.
